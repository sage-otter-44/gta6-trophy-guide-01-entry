Roadmap to the platinum

Built from the official descriptions: which story chapters and missions, which collectibles and counts, in what order. Cheats off, manual save at the start of every chapter — there is no mission replay.

    Missable trophies

    1 trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude. Lucia’s balcony meditation at Jason’s house in Chapter 1 counts toward it and is reported to disappear once that chapter ends. Do it in Chapter 1. Stranger mission chains are also reported to have windows tied to story progress, so start them as soon as they unlock. Everything else can be earned after the story.

    Sources: OutputLag; GTABase and PowerPyx via EGamers.io; TechWiser via EGamers.io.

      About this guide

      Names, descriptions, grades and PSN progress targets are the official list as PSNProfiles and Exophase publish it (checked 2026-11-19). Every guide is written in this entry’s own words and names its sources beside it; where no unlock method was confirmed at launch, the guide says so instead of guessing. All 55 trophies are single-player and offline — there are no online trophies. This guide works with the network off and stores your progress only in this browser (localStorage); nothing is uploaded anywhere.
